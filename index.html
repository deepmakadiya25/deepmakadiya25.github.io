About Me

I am a post-doctoral fellow in the Department of Mathematics at Bar-Ilan University, working with Prof. Elena I. Bunina on the automorphisms of twisted Chevalley groups over local and commutative rings. I completed my Ph.D. at IIT Bombay under Prof. Shripad M. Garge, on the structure of twisted Chevalley groups.

My interests include algebraic groups, Chevalley groups & algebras, Lie groups & algebras, and representation theory. Please feel free to explore my research and publications, or to contact me with any questions or possible collaborations.
