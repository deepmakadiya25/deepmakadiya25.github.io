What is this site?

This is the academic website of Deep H. Makadiya.

The site is divided into two main parts. The first highlights my academic profile and professional background, including my education, positions, research interests, publications, teaching, and other academic activities. It is designed to provide colleagues and collaborators with a clear, concise overview of my scholarly work.

The second part provides a broader perspective beyond my formal academic record. It features a more personal introduction, photographs, news and announcements, selected writings, and other interests and activities that fall outside the scope of a traditional CV.

Together, these two parts provide both a structured account of my academic work and a broader perspective on the person behind it. They also share the same look and rhythm, and each is always just a click away from the other.

Did you know?

You can customize the site’s appearance using the Appearance button, at the bottom of the side menu or on the right of the top menu. It lets you change the colour, the typeface and the theme (light or dark), as well as the overall layout on the main pages. The site remembers your preferences.

You can also reach the photo gallery by clicking the photograph on the home page.

Pressing the / key on any page opens the search. Typing # in the search brings up the available tags—publication, talk, news, photo, and the rest—so you can narrow the search to a particular type of content.
