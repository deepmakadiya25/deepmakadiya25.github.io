Useful Features

Search

Open site search using the button at the top right, or press / on your keyboard. Search for a topic, paper, talk or name, then use the filters to narrow the results.

With search open, use ↑ and ↓ to select a result, Enter to open the selected result, and Esc to close search.

Jump to a section

The contents button at the bottom right lists the sections on the current page. Choose one to jump directly to it. The up arrow beside it takes you back to the top.

Switch between light and dark mode

Use the light/dark button in the top menu. Your preference is remembered as you move between pages.

Filter news and blog posts

Use the category buttons on News & Updates, Blogs and Past Announcements to show the entries you’re interested in. The homepage shows up to five latest news items and posts; the complete lists are on their own pages.

Explore the photographs

The homepage portrait links to the Photo Gallery. Select a gallery image to enlarge it and read its caption.

A new quote each day

Visit Quote of the Day on the homepage for the quotation selected for that date.
